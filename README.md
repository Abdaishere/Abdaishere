## Main quest

**Software Engineer at Odoo, Dubai.** April 2025 to now.

I work with the client to find out what the business actually needs, map it to what Odoo already does, and write the data model and the delivery plan before the code. Custom code only where it's needed. Python and PostgreSQL, on 100+ client projects across the Gulf, mostly stock and accounting.

- **Stock aging.** I designed and built the first version of a stock aging layer. The report used to walk the whole stock list on every render. Now it renders in under a second.
- **Billing for a logistics client.** The biggest system I've built: postpaid billing for storage and handling, prepaid for rented floor space. It arrived as a vague request, so I cut it into small pieces and agreed each one with the client.
- **Other people's code.** I review partner developers' code against Odoo's standards, and I've coached two partners.

## Side quests

<a href="https://abdaishere.com/polyball/play/"><img alt="PolyBall, a rhythm game I made alone. Every note you hear, you played. Live gameplay next to the home screen." src="readme/card-polyball.gif" width="100%"></a>

I designed, built and released it alone. It's been on Google Play since 13 November 2026, and it runs in the browser with nothing to install.

- **Played live, never recorded.** Every note is played at runtime: 16 instruments, up to 12 notes at once.
- **Leaderboard** on Supabase (PostgreSQL), with a monthly cup and anti-cheat on the server.
- **GitHub Actions** builds six targets, and bots play 24 runs per pass with deliberate mistakes.
- **15 languages**, with the Arabic right-to-left layout covered by tests.

**[Play it in your browser](https://abdaishere.com/polyball/play/)** · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.polyball.game) · [How it's built](https://abdaishere.com/polyball/) · The source is private.

<a href="https://github.com/Abdaishere/monitor-buddy"><img alt="Monitor Buddy, a tiny desk screen with a face. Its face going through the moods of a workday: locked in, CI failed, on air, lunch break. A polaroid of the real one. A fork of IdefixRC's Monitor-Buddy." src="readme/card-buddy.svg" width="100%"></a>

It goes red when CI fails, says ON AIR while the mic is on, and chews during lunch. It's a fork of [IdefixRC's Monitor-Buddy](https://github.com/IdefixRC/Monitor-Buddy): the base firmware, the web flasher and the printed holder are their work. I added the workday features (CI, calls, calendar, lunch), a laptop app, and a simulator: the real firmware running on a PC, where a whole workday passes in seconds. My keyboard dock, built on their holder, came off my printer.

[Repository](https://github.com/Abdaishere/monitor-buddy) (MIT) · [The write-up](https://abdaishere.com/prints/monitor-buddy/)

<br>

<a href="https://github.com/Abdaishere/E-Jam"><img alt="E-Jam, my graduation project: a test bench for network switches. The Flutter admin client's dashboard, and one stream walking through its states." src="readme/card-ejam.svg" width="100%"></a>

It runs RFC 2544 and RFC 2889 benchmarks. My graduation project at Cairo University, built in collaboration with Siemens EDA, whose engineers were our mentors, sponsor and product owner. Five of us built it.

I wrote Center Point, the control plane, in Rust on Actix Web: a REST API that moves every stream, device and process through explicit state machines and reads the test statistics from Apache Kafka. The strip on the card is one stream's trip. I also built the Flutter admin client, the dashboard in the picture. 256 commits, the most on the team.

[Repository](https://github.com/Abdaishere/E-Jam) (MIT) · [Project page](https://abdaishere.com/e-jam/)
